# Image assets

`social/` contains two owner-supplied photos and six owner-supplied MP4 clips from Infiniti Boss Auto's social media. The social photos feature the orange Infiniti G37 in Jamaica; these business-provided assets are used in the hero and “A Closer Look” gallery.

`brand-logo.jpeg` is the original business logo supplied by the owner. `brand-logo-450.jpg` is a 454-pixel-wide, quality-88 derivative used in the header, footer and favicon; it is about 64% smaller than the original. The original is preserved unchanged.

- `workshop-detail.jpg`: https://unsplash.com/photos/cars-being-repaired-in-a-workshop-VdXnpwrp1qc
- `car-wash-rims.jpg`: Unsplash photo by Zac Nielson, https://unsplash.com/photos/person-cleaning-car-tire-with-brush-8k_T1EwTySs
- `bar-drinks.jpg`: Unsplash photo, https://unsplash.com/photos/clear-drinking-glass-with-orange-liquid-on-brown-wooden-table-Z40sav8IYqQ

Files in `social/` are copied from user-provided Instagram downloads. Each `clip-0N-thumb.jpg` is a unique frame captured from its matching `clip-0N.mp4`; the numbered labels are used because descriptive post titles were not supplied. Videos use native HTML playback controls and `preload="none"` so the large files are not downloaded until a visitor chooses to play one.

The car-wash and bar images are illustrative stock photos, not photos of Infiniti Boss Auto or its on-site facilities. They are stored locally for reliable display.

The six supplied videos total approximately 82 MB. Keep the supplied social media attribution/context with these assets if the site is handed off.
